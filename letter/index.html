A letter for the teacher who believed in you

Every Teachers' Day, we thank teachers in speeches. This time, each of us writes one real letter, and our officers place it in a teacher's hands.

Why we watched this film

In Big World, Chunhe dreams of becoming a teacher while the people around him doubt he can. A dream like that survives because someone believes in it. Teachers do the same for us, often before we believe in ourselves.

Why a letter

Teachers' Day is for saying what we usually forget to say. A teacher can keep a letter for years.

What happens next

You write to one teacher, which takes about five minutes. The officers print your letter exactly as you wrote it and deliver it by hand.
